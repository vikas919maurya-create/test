Enterprise Security

Your Farm is Completely Invisible to the Internet

Most remote access tools expose your miners to the open internet — creating serious security risks. MonkGo works the opposite way.

The MonkGo Client creates an outbound-only encrypted connection from your farm to Cloudflare's global network. Your router never opens a single incoming port. Your miners' real IP addresses are never visible to anyone outside your farm.

🔒
Zero Open Ports Your router firewall stays 100% closed. No incoming connections accepted.
🛡️

Hidden Miner URLs Dashboard never stores miner addresses in HTML. They exist only in RAM, invisible to browser DevTools.
